### Cost Traceability

Every product COGS is calculated as:
```
COGS = Σ (material_quantity × material_unit_cost) + labor + tooling_amortization
```

Each material quantity is specified in the product's BOM. Each unit cost is
quoted by the named supplier. Labor and tooling estimates are based on
industry benchmarks for similar manufacturing processes.

## Cost Calculation Integrity

### COGS Calculation Method

For each product, COGS is calculated at production scale (5,000-50,000 units/year):

1. **Material costs:** Σ(quantity × supplier_quote_price)
2. **Labor costs:** industry_benchmark_hours × burdened_rate
3. **Tooling amortization:** total_NRE / projected_units_over_5_years
4. **Overhead:** 15% of material + labor (standard manufacturing rate)
5. **Total COGS:** material + labor + amortization + overhead

### Margin Calculation

```
Gross Margin % = (MSRP - COGS) / MSRP × 100
```

All margins in this document fall within 33-57%, consistent with specialty
manufacturing benchmarks. No product has a margin below 30%.

### Revenue Calculation

```
Revenue = Units_Sold × MSRP
Profit = Revenue × Gross_Margin %
```

Year 1 projections use 50% of optimistic estimates (conservative scenario).
Year 2+ projections use 75% of optimistic estimates.
